Put each store's picture in this folder, named exactly:

  dayen.jpg     -> Dayen's Catering Services
  3m.jpg        -> 3M's Catering Services
  nataran.jpg   -> Nataran Naval (Nataran Catering Events Services)

The website uses them automatically as the background of the caterer card,
the package/menu cards, the business page banner, the compare table header
and the seller dashboard buttons. Landscape photos around 1280px wide work best.
Until a file exists, a navy/sea gradient is shown instead.
(A seller can also upload a photo from Seller > Business profile; that one is
saved in that browser only, so the images/ folder is the way to show it to everyone.)
